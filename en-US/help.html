3D models, animation & printable flashcards

Learn how to explore animal and plant models in your browser, play available animations, print nature flashcards, and use Little Nature offline or in AR.

How do I find an animal or plant model?

Browse the catalog or search by a common name in any of the six supported languages, or by scientific name where available. Filter by category, theme, or animated models.

Models

Can I view 3D models without installing the app?

Yes. Open a model page and choose View in 3D, then rotate or zoom to inspect it. Some models use our hosted viewer; others open an embedded Sketchfab viewer and contact that third-party service. An internet connection is required.

Models

Which models have animation?

Look for the animation label or open the animated collection. On models with playback controls, play or pause the animation and compare poses. These animations are visual illustrations, not measurements of real animal movement.

How do I print animal and plant flashcards?

Add models to study cards, choose A4 or Letter paper, and select picture-and-name cards or matching picture and name cards. Scientific names are optional. Print from the browser or choose Save as PDF if your browser offers it. Selections stay in this browser.

Does the app support offline viewing and AR?

Little Nature on iPhone, iPad, and Mac supports 3D previews and on-demand downloads. Downloaded models can be viewed offline; use Download Manager to remove them. AR requires a compatible iPhone or iPad and camera permission. Follow the placement guidance and use gestures to move, scale, or rotate the model. Mac supports 3D viewing, not camera-based AR.

Can I reuse the model files and learning materials?

Catalog models are provided under CC0. Each model page links to its source and creator. Reuse the files under that license, and credit the source where practical. Taxonomy and common names may be updated; the pages are learning aids, not species-identification or scientific measurement tools.

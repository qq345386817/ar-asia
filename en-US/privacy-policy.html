Little Nature

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Little Nature is designed as a local browsing and AR viewing app for biological model records. The app does not require an account and does not upload your photos, camera feed, or downloaded model files.

Privacy at a glance

No account required

You do not need to register, log in, or provide a personal profile to use the app.

Camera used only for AR

Camera access is requested only when you use AR viewing features on supported iPhone or iPad devices.

Downloaded models stay local

Model files you download are stored on your device so they can be opened later, including offline.

No advertising SDKs

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell personal information.

Information the app accesses

Camera

The camera is used to detect your surroundings and place AR models. Camera frames are not uploaded by Little Nature.

Local storage

The app uses local storage for downloaded model files, language preferences, onboarding state, and download status.

Network access

Network access is used to retrieve the model manifest and download model files from the configured content delivery endpoint.

Anonymous product analytics

The app sends first-party product interaction events such as catalog views, model details, downloads, 3D previews, and AR starts. Data may include random installation and session identifiers, app and build versions, OS version, locale, and fixed model attributes. Identifiers are HMAC-hashed before storage. Analytics contains no search terms, camera content, downloaded files, account details, or free-form text and is not used for advertising or tracking. Cloudflare provides the endpoint and database infrastructure.

Data retention and deletion

Downloaded model files

Downloaded model files remain on your device until you delete them in Download Manager or remove the app.

Support requests

If you contact support by email, your message will be used only to respond to your request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Using this website

Study-card selections are saved only in this browser. We collect limited first-party usage events such as successful 3D loads, animations, printing, and App Store link clicks, with a random tab-session identifier, language, device class, and fixed model or theme identifiers. We do not collect search text, selected card contents, camera images, or full page URLs. Session identifiers are hashed before storage and events are retained for up to 90 days. Analytics respects Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Opening the Sketchfab viewer connects directly to that third-party service; its privacy policy applies to those requests.
